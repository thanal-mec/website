# Project memory

## Product intent & audience

- Website with a nature/environmental/sustainability theme (evidenced by "forest-green," "canopy," "initiative areas," "annual reports" sections).
- Homepage is a primary landing surface with multiple content sections (hero, initiatives, annual reports, footer).

## Brand personality & voice

- Natural, organic, premium feel; communicates environmental stewardship.
- Visual tone leans calming and earthy rather than corporate.

## Visual design & UX direction

- **Palette:** nature-green base, forest-green (deep), light canopy greens, ivory (content/contrast), amber (accent), dark forest (text/contrast).
- **Gradient treatment:** layered gradients preferred over flat fills — soft full-page nature-green canvas, richer forest wash behind hero, radial canopy-style highlights, lighter green panels for report/initiative sections, matching green footer.
- **Readability:** preserve ivory, amber, and dark-forest contrast against green backgrounds.
- **Patterns:** section transitions use gradient shifts rather than hard boundaries.

## Decisions, constraints & rationale

- **Stack:** Vite + React, JSX, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React icons. No extra UI/icon packages.
- **Path alias:** `@/` maps to `src/`.
- **Gradient approach chosen** for visual depth and nature feel while maintaining content legibility.

## Open questions & assumptions

- Homepage gradient is implemented; other pages may or may not need matching treatment — not yet requested.
- Exact gradient colors/angles may need refinement against real content and the reference image.
- No explicit typography or spacing system established yet.
